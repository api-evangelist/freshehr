# freshEHR Clinical Informatics

> freshEHR Clinical Informatics Ltd is a UK open-standards health and social care
> informatics consultancy, founded in 2014 by Dr Ian McNicoll and registered in
> England as company 08989238. It sells expertise, not software: openEHR strategy,
> clinical information modelling (archetypes and templates), terminology and data
> mapping, implementation guidance, training and mentoring. It operates no API of
> its own — no developer portal, no public API reference, no machine-readable
> contract on any host it controls.

## What freshEHR does

- **Clinical informatics** — openEHR strategy, analysis, design, development,
  governance and curation of openEHR clinical models.
- **Training and mentoring** — online, face-to-face and mentored programmes in
  openEHR clinical modelling, including "Introduction to openEHR Clinical
  Modelling" and "Advanced openEHR Clinical Modelling".
- **Implementation support** — data and terminology mapping and technical guidance
  across openEHR, SNOMED CT, dm+d, ICD, HL7 V2, HL7 CDA and HL7 FHIR.

Named clients and partners on the company site include NHS England, NHS Education
for Scotland, Digital Health and Care Wales, HSE Ireland, SNEE ICB, One London,
CatSalut (Catalonia), the Jamaican Ministry of Health and Wellness, Karolinska
University Hospital, DaVita, Better Care, TietoEvry, Apperta Foundation, Toukan
Labs, Ethical Healthcare and UCL.

## Documentation freshEHR publishes

These are freshEHR-authored teaching sites about the **openEHR standard and
third-party openEHR platforms**. None of them documents a freshEHR API.

- [openEHR-101 — openEHR client-side development](https://freshehr.github.io/openEHR-101/):
  an introduction to the openEHR REST API and the Ehrscape platform, covering
  archetypes and templates, the openEHR Reference Model, using Postman against
  Ehrscape, EHR Explorer, and CKM.
- [openEHR Practical Modelling Examples](https://freshehr.github.io/training/):
  worked modelling exercises (problem/diagnosis, adverse reaction risk, medication
  order, vital signs, Clinical Frailty Scale).
- [openEHR clinical modelling tooling setup](https://freshehr.github.io/openehr_tool_docs/):
  step-by-step setup of an openEHR modelling toolchain.
- [freshEHR on GitHub](https://github.com/freshehr): openEHR archetypes, templates,
  training material, tooling and experiments.

## API surface

freshEHR has **no API**. Verified 2026-09-02:

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, gRPC/Protobuf or WSDL contract on any
  freshEHR-controlled host.
- No `/.well-known/*` document served on freshehr.com or freshehr.github.io — every
  named path returned 404. See `well-known/freshehr-well-known.yml`.
- No A2A agent card and no MCP server operated by freshEHR.
- `api.freshehr.com`, `docs.freshehr.com`, `training.freshehr.com` and
  `app.freshehr.com` do not resolve in DNS.

The openEHR REST API that freshEHR teaches and consults on is a specification of the
[openEHR Foundation](https://openehr.org/), not a freshEHR product. Do not attribute
it to freshEHR.
